Fantasy Sportsbook

A play-money sportsbook for my fantasy football league. Explore matchup bets, player markets, and the leaderboard.

Play money · Approved league access

Enter the Sportsbook
About this projectTry the public demo
The problem & audience
I wanted to give my dynasty league another way to compete and spend time together beyond the fantasy standings.
What I built
A sportsbook experience for matchup bets, player performances, season futures, ticket history, and league standings.
A design decision
Use play money so participation does not require risking real money. Keep the public demo separate from private league access.
Try it now
Explore fictional markets, sample tickets, and the leaderboard in the demo. Betting, account changes, and email delivery are disabled there.
Current limits
The league app requires an approved email mapped to a team by the league administrator. The demo is illustrative; live line updates are still in development.

NCAAB Betting Model

Compare Division I basketball models with historical matchups, recorded sportsbook lines, and results.

Public preview

Open the NCAAB Model
About this projectExplore historical winner performance
The problem & audience
Basketball fans exploring models need to see what was evaluated and what the evidence can actually support.
What I am building
A place to explore Division I data, historical model comparisons, recorded prices, and evaluation methods.
A design decision
Separate winner predictions from spread-cover predictions, and show uncertainty and source limitations alongside results.
Try it now
Compare historical spread models or explore winner performance using the link above.
Current limits
This model is in public preview. Historical comparisons were not issued as live bets. No validated spread betting edge has been established; winner accuracy is not a betting return.

A little about me

Family, friends, and sports are a big part of my life. I love watching, playing, and talking about sports, and turning data that might otherwise sit in a spreadsheet into something people can interact with and enjoy.

I started these projects to see how far curiosity, passion, and perseverance could take me. Building something I can call my own is rewarding. Sharing it with others is the best part. The topics may change, but making data enjoyable and useful will always be the focus.
