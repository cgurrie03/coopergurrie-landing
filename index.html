A little about me

Family, friends, and sports are a big part of my life. I love watching, playing, and talking about sports, and turning data that might otherwise sit in a spreadsheet into something people can interact with and enjoy.

I started these projects to see how far curiosity, passion, and perseverance could take me. Building something I can call my own is rewarding. Sharing it with others is the best part. The topics may change, but making data enjoyable and useful will always be the focus.

Sportsbook

A play-money sportsbook for my fantasy football dynasty league. Bet on matchups, player performances, and season outcomes, then see how you stack up against your leaguemates.

Live for select users

Open sportsbook
About this project

I built this to give my dynasty league another way to compete and spend time together, using play money so nobody has to risk losing real money to join in.

  • Matchup bets: predict winners, total scores, and winning margins.
  • Player bets: predict individual fantasy performances.
  • Weekly predictions: bet on outcomes across the league.
  • Season futures: predict the champion, No. 1 pick, and playoff teams.
  • League activity: view your bets, everyone else's bets, and the season leaderboard.

Today it's built for one league. Longer term, I'd like to bring that extra layer of competition and interaction to other fantasy leagues.

NBA model

An NBA model designed to find potential edges in sportsbook lines, starting with spreads. Explore daily matchup recommendations and compare odds once it launches.

In development

About this project

The NBA counterpart to my college basketball model, built around current and historical NBA data. It will start with spreads, with winners and totals also planned.

Planned features

  • Daily game recommendations with matchup information.
  • Compare model projections with lines from major sportsbooks.
  • Highlight the best available odds among the books compared for a recommended bet.
  • Keep a history of recommendations and their results to evaluate and refine the model.

The model and its website are both in development.
